# User Updates and Escalation Communication

## Acknowledgement
"Your issue has been logged under [fictional ticket ID]. We are reviewing the details and will provide an update through the agreed channel."

## Progress update
"We have completed the initial checks. The issue is not yet resolved, and we are continuing with the next approved diagnostic step."

## Escalation update
"The issue requires investigation by [support team]. I have shared the symptoms, affected service, impact, checks performed and relevant observations. Please do not send passwords or verification codes."

## Waiting for user
"We need [specific non-sensitive information] to continue. Please reply through the approved support channel. The ticket will remain pending until we receive it."

## Resolution update
"The reported issue appears to be resolved based on [actual verification performed]. Please confirm that the service is working as expected."

## Handoff note structure
- Reason for escalation
- User/business impact
- Scope: one user, team, or multiple users
- Exact error and time observed
- Checks performed and results
- Actions already taken
- What assistance is requested from the receiving team
- User communication already provided
