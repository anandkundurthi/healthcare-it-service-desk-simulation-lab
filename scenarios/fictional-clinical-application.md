# Scenario: Fictional Clinical Application Support

## Scenario
A fictional clinic employee reports that a training-only clinical application displays "Unable to load schedule" after sign-in. This is a fabricated application and contains no real patient data.

## Intake questions
- What is the application name and version?
- What exact message appears?
- When did the issue begin?
- Is the issue affecting one user, one workstation, one department, or multiple departments?
- Does the user have access to other approved applications?
- Has anything changed recently, such as an update or workstation change?

## Safe first-level checks
1. Record the exact error and time reported.
2. Establish the number of affected users and business impact.
3. Check basic workstation and network symptoms within support scope.
4. Follow the fictional application's knowledge article, if provided by the lab.
5. Do not access, create, copy, or request real patient records.
6. Do not change clinical data or attempt a workaround that could affect patient care.

## Escalation
Escalate immediately according to local policy if the issue may affect clinical operations, safety, data integrity, or multiple users. In a real healthcare setting, follow the organization's clinical downtime and incident procedures.

## Practice note
This scenario is for support workflow rehearsal only. It is not clinical advice or a real clinical system procedure.
