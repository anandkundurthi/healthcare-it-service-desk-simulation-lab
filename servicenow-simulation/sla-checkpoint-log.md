## Checkpoint review
- Was the ticket acknowledged within the applicable target?
- Was the requester updated when promised?
- Was the ticket routed before the escalation threshold?
- Were delays and dependencies documented?
- Was the actual policy consulted?

This worksheet demonstrates SLA awareness; it does not establish or promise any specific SLA.
